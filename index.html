What happens when you ask Ask-Your-PDF a question

  1. pdf → chunksPages are split into overlapping pieces
  2. MiniLM embeddingsEach piece becomes a vector
  3. FAISS searchThe closest pieces to your question are found
  4. Groq LLMAnswers only from those pieces
  5. answer + page numbersSo you can check the source

Try it: ask-your-pdf-genai.streamlit.app

About

I’m Rohit, a BCA student from Lucknow who builds AI projects.

I’m in my first year of BCA at IGNOU, studying in distance mode. I’m teaching myself AI and machine learning on my own, with a 6-month plan I wrote myself: Python, maths, machine learning, deep learning, and now generative AI.

So far I have built 11 projects, and every one is a working app you can open. They include a delivery-delay predictor, a credit card fraud detector, a chest X-ray classifier, a support-ticket router, a banking chatbot that understands Hindi, English and Hinglish, and an app that answers questions from your PDF.

I train the models in Python with scikit-learn and PyTorch, and build the apps in Streamlit. When a score looks too good, I check for data leakage. I put the real numbers on each project, including the weak ones. For example, my fraud model finds only about a third of fraud cases, and I say so.

I also use AI coding tools every day, and I read and test what they write before I use it. Right now I’m learning FastAPI, Docker and AI agents. I’m looking for an AI/ML or data science internship.
